How FuelConnect Works

Our goal is to make it easier for buyers and suppliers to discover business opportunities.

1

Discover

Buyers explore petroleum product categories and available suppliers.

2

Connect

Buyers and suppliers can connect and discuss their business requirements.

3

Verify

Suppliers must undergo appropriate licence and business verification before participating.

Important Notice

FuelConnect Nigeria is a digital marketplace connecting buyers with independent suppliers. It does not claim to be a petroleum seller, distributor, or NNPC partner. All suppliers are expected to meet applicable regulatory requirements.
